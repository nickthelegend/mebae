Mebae 芽生え

Pin each habit to the real place it happens. Pinch the sprout when you do it, and it grows. Come back tomorrow and it is still there.

  1. Pinch a seed in the tray on your desk.
  2. Pick an icon, point at the spot (a doorway, the counter, your desk) and pinch.
  3. Pinch the sprout each time you do the habit. Hold 1 s to undo.

Everything stays in this browser (IndexedDB). No account, no server. Hands only: no controllers needed.
