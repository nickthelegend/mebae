Growing Notes & FAQ

Questions, answered by the garden

Why pin habits to real places?

The place is the reminder. You pass the doorway and see the moss you grew by stretching there. Mebae uses one WebXR anchor per habit, so the sprout stays on that real spot between sessions.

Why only six habits?

Quest keeps up to 8 persistent anchors per site. Six habits plus the desk tray uses 7, so there is always room to move one.

What if a sprout loses its spot?

If an anchor can't be restored, the sprout waits in the tray with "Pin again". It never jumps to a guessed position.

What happens when I miss a day?

Growth pauses, and the plant rests. It never dies and never shrinks. Five good days in a week bring a bloom.

Where is my data?

Only in your headset's browser (IndexedDB). No account, no server. Export and import are plain JSON.

Do I need controllers?

No. Everything is a pinch: hand ray for far spots, a poke for near ones. On Meta VR Glasses, look and pinch.
